Entry to the FTB Discord is bonded. Post a 1000 FTB bond, verify your deposit, and you're in. The bond is your skin in the game: if you scam a member, the community can file an accusation and vote to liquidate and burn your bond. Honest actors get their bond back on a clean exit; scammers lose it to the fire.
