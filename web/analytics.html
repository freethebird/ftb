not shown here — and why
Page visits & unique users — not measured. The site is static, pinned on IPFS, and runs no tracker; nothing about your visit leaves your browser. Honest by architecture, in keeping with the project's stealth posture.

Trade volumes, transaction counts, per-category breakdowns, full price history — ThorChain-style panels like these are served by an indexer (ThorChain runs Midgard) that aggregates chain history off a backend. FTB deliberately has no indexer and no server. These light up only if a self-hosted, privacy-respecting indexer is added — a separate infrastructure decision, not a front-end one.
