swapn3 ⇆ neo x · atomic · no bridge

Move assets between FTB's homes with a hash-timelock swap. No bridge, no wrapped token, no custody — a secret and two timers. The solver locks on the far chain first; you commit only after you have verified that lock on-chain. If anything stalls, you refund after your own timelock. Your funds are never at a counterparty's mercy.
